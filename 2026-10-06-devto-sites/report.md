# What dev.to authors show on their own sites (2026-10-06)

Sample: 100 authors of popular dev.to articles (past year, 10 tags) who list a personal website; 94 after removing 6 org/agency/tip-jar sites. Sources: the public dev.to API and one GET of each homepage. LinkedIn was not scraped (its terms forbid it); work status is only what authors say themselves.

## dev.to activity
Median 37 articles per author (quartiles 19 / 37 / 86; max 2,007). Samples drawn from popular articles skew toward active writers.

## What their sites show
Two measures: strict = a menu item/link/heading with that name; loose = the word anywhere on the homepage. The truth is between them (many sites are one-page apps whose sections have no menu link; 22 sites had no recognisable section at all, some of them JavaScript-rendered, so the strict count misses them).

| Section | strict | loose |
|---|---|---|
| Blog / articles | 51% | 86% |
| Projects / portfolio / repos | 32% | 74% |
| Contact / hire me | 40% | 38% (services/hire wording) |
| Talks / videos / podcast | 16% | 61% (loose is inflated by "video"/"posts") |
| Services | 13% | — |
| Uses / now / TIL | 10% | 7% |
| Resume / CV | 5% | 27% |
| Case studies | — | 11% |

- 39 of 94 sites have neither a blog nor a projects section in the menu: a one-page "about me + links".
- Links out: GitHub profile 58, LinkedIn 56, specific repositories 30, dev.to 26 of the 48 who also run their own blog.

## What they say about work (self-stated, bio + homepage)
Freelance/independent 8 · student/bootcamp 6 · founder/CEO/CTO (explicit title) 5 · "currently at <company>" 4 · open to work / available for hire 2. Most authors state nothing about employment on their site.

## Caveats
Keyword and menu matching on the homepage only; JS-rendered sites are under-counted; sample = popular authors, not all developers.
